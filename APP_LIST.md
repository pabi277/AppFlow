# App portfolio: official Play Store names

Use this central register for every app in the portfolio. Record the official
Google Play title exactly as it appears in Play Console or on the live listing,
including capitalization and punctuation. Keep any working name separate; use
`Not set` until the official title is confirmed. If localized listing titles
differ, add one row per locale. Do not invent an official name or copy this
portfolio file into an individual app repository.

Update the package ID, public listing URL, lifecycle status, and verification
date when they become known or change. Status examples: `Proposed`, `Building`,
`Internal test`, `Closed test`, `Production`, and `Retired`. Never put account
credentials or personal data here.
